01 · ABOUT CHENGDU SHADOW PUPPETRY

Chengdu Shadow Puppetry

Chengdu shadow puppetry is a traditional folk art of Sichuan. Carved and coloured figures are brought to life between light and screen.

Its colours, patterns, faces and costumes form a visual language: one that reveals character, identity and atmosphere.
